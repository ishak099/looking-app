Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to the Looking app, all versions

In short: Looking does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, with anyone, ever. There is no account, no server, and no analytics anywhere in the app. Everything below explains exactly what that means.

What the app stores, and where

Looking has no backend and makes no network requests of any kind while you use it. A small amount of information is saved directly on your device, using your operating system's standard local storage, so the app can remember your settings between sessions:

  • whether you've completed the opening screen once
  • your notification preferences (frequency and quiet hours)
  • your position in the shuffled sequence of pointers, so none repeat until you've seen them all
  • whether you've been asked for notification permission, and a small counter used only to time that one-time request

None of this ever leaves your device. It is not sent to us, because we have no server to send it to. It is not sent to any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Notifications

Looking can schedule local notifications so a pointer occasionally arrives at an unpredictable time. These are scheduled entirely on your device by your device's operating system. No push notification service, push token, or remote server is involved at any point.

No accounts, no analytics, no third parties

There is nothing to sign up for. The app contains no analytics software, no advertising software, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party libraries that transmit data anywhere. We do not know how many times you've opened the app, which pointers you've seen, or anything else about your use of it — and we've built it so that we couldn't find out even if we wanted to.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. Looking is not directed at children and contains no features that gather any personal information from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated first, and the change will be reflected in the app's version history.
